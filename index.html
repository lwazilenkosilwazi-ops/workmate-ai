Using AI responsibly

  • You stay in control. Every result is a draft. Nothing is sent or saved without you.
  • Privacy first. Emails, phone numbers and long ID numbers are masked by default. No conversations are stored on our server.
  • No made-up sources. The research tool is told not to invent citations. Verify important facts yourself.
  • Treat pasted text as data. Instructions hidden in pasted emails or transcripts are ignored.
  • Don't paste secrets. Avoid passwords, payment details, health records and confidential client data.
